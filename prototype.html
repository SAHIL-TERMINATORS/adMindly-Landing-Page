Design notes carried across every screen
Persistent nav between Copilot, Monitoring, Competitors and Calendar.
Copilot is the core differentiator — its states get the most detail: empty, suggestion given, edit applied, edit reverted.
Consistent card patterns across Monitoring & Competitors — metric cards, list rows.
Two connection states everywhere: connected / not connected.
Stub screens are fine for Calendar, Notifications and team roles in this prototype.
Out of scope — a full in-app photo/video editor. Edit-and-regenerate via chat is the differentiator.
