FAQ

The questions we get first

Does Admindly edit the video or image itself?

It regenerates the creative with the change applied — a reorder, a caption, a hook, a headline. A full in-app photo/video editor is out of scope; edit-and-regenerate through chat is the differentiator.

Which platforms can I connect?

Instagram and Facebook today, with a per-platform OAuth step and a connection status you'll see across the app. TikTok and YouTube are on the roadmap.

Is my data used to train models?

Placeholder answer for the prototype: no. Your creatives and metrics stay in your workspace and aren't used for training.

Do I need a card to start?

No. Starter is free forever. Growth has a 14-day trial and you add a card only if you keep it.

Can my team collaborate?

Scale adds roles and an approval flow — marketer → approver → brand manager — so an edit is reviewed before it goes live. Approvals surface in the Notifications Center.

Is this a finished product?

This is a v1 prototype. The copy and the numbers are placeholders; the screens and flows are real. Open the live demo to click through all nine.
